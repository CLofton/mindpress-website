About MindPress

An AI-agent company that installs production systems.

MindPress exists to move companies past experiments. We pick 1–2 workflows and install working systems on your stack. We do not fly. The front door is a written diagnostic.

Origin

Built from operating complexity, not AI novelty.

MindPress was founded by Chris Lofton after years of operating large education programs across hundreds of locations and instructors. At that scale, every back-office delay compounds: payroll, reporting, hiring, forecasting, compliance, and management cadence all become leverage points.

The insight is direct: the most valuable AI work is inside the repeatable processes that already run the company. Those workflows need agents on the system of record, controls, and adoption—not a workshop and not a demo that never leaves a sandbox.

Sister brand
