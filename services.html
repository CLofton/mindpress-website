Offers

Six places a diagnostic usually lands.

Buyer titles are listed so the right executive can own the work. You still start with one engagement, not six projects.

CRO

Revenue workflows

For commercial operators who need agents and automations on the revenue path—wired to CRM, CPQ, billing, and the people who already own the deal.

  • Quote-to-cash, deal desk, and pipeline hygiene
  • Handoffs between sales, CS, and finance
  • Not outbound SEO, ads, or social campaigns
COO · CFO

Operations and back-office

For operators who run the company: close, payables, hiring, reporting, and the repeatable work that compounds when it is slow.

  • Agents plus systems of record, with review paths
  • Exception routing, audit trails, and named owners
  • Finance, people, and operating cadence—not a chatbot layer
CCO · VP Support

Customer-facing agents

For customer, support, and success leaders. Voice, chat, and email that call APIs and resolve work—Sierra- and Decagon-shaped, not a FAQ widget.

  • Live actions against tickets, orders, accounts, and billing
  • Identity, permissions, and human escalation
  • Measured on resolution and quality, not chat volume
CIO

Knowledge and systems of record

For technology leaders who need connectors, identity, and retrieval over ERP, CRM, DMS, and warehouse. KPI monitoring and AI reporting live here.

  • Identity, permissions, and integration fabric
  • RAG over documents and tables operators already trust
  • Reporting and watchers as a byproduct, not a separate SKU
Quality

Quality and governance

For leaders who will not put an unmeasured model in front of customers or the ledger. Evals, guardrails, tracing, and human-in-the-loop.

  • Evaluation harnesses and regression tests for the workflow
  • Tracing, logging, and review queues
  • Policy, thresholds, and kill switches before scale
Adoption

Adoption and operating change

For teams that have watched tools land and die. Diagnostic, enablement, runbooks, and a named operator so the system is used after go-live.

  • Operator training, SOPs, and on-call paths
  • Change management with the people who do the work
  • Not a custom AI training program sold as a peer SKU
What we will not do

Clear edges. No retainers in disguise.

MindPress is an AI-agent company. If the work is marketing, a workshop, or training a foundation model, we will say no. We do not fly.

Workshops
