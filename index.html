Customer-facing agents

Voice, chat, and email agents that call your APIs. Sierra- and Decagon-shaped work: resolve, route, and act inside the tools the customer already uses.

Method

Map. Install. Optimize.

Every engagement moves from diagnosis to a working production system, with executive clarity at each step.

01

Map

Two weeks. We identify 1–2 high-frequency workflows, decision points, systems of record, owners, and what “done” means.

02

Install

Paid production install. We wire agents and automations into your systems of record, write operating procedures, and test exception paths.

03

Optimize

Evals, tracing, adoption, cycle time, and error rates. The system improves after launch instead of rotting in a demo.

Trust model

Engineering discipline for systems that have to hold.
